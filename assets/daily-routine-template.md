# Daily Routine Template · #protocol-routine

Post **before market open**, every trading day. The Discord timestamp is the
proof of filing. Criterion 03 (Falsifiability Protocol v2, Article V) scores the
first three lines; the fourth is a captured reminder, not part of the pass/fail bar.

A line counts as done only with an explicit **✅** (or ✓ / `[x]` / "done").
Use ❌ or omit a line to mark it skipped.

---

## Standard day

```
PRE-MARKET ROUTINE · M/D/YYYY
Phelps: ✅ rehearsed both endpoints — the winning case and the adverse case
Predisposal: ✅ key levels + pre-decided responses to today's decision points
Scaling: ✅ buffer ceiling restated (e.g. Buffer 2 → 2 ES max); scale-down law set
Abundance: ✅ ~40 IMO + 20 MO opportunities a month — patience is the edge; no forced trades, the targets get met
```

- **IMO** = Intraday Majority Opinion · **MO** = Majority Opinion
- The Abundance line is the daily reminder: the month supplies the opportunities;
  forcing a trade is never required to hit the targets.

## Day you are fully out (excluded from adherence, not a miss)

```
NO SESSION · M/D/YYYY · <reason>
```

Reasons: `market holiday` · `personal` · `FOMC sit-out` · `stand-down`.
Weekends and exchange holidays are excluded automatically — no post needed.

---

## Scoring (Criterion 03)

- **Complete day** = Phelps ✅ + Predisposal ✅ + Scaling ✅ (Abundance is captured
  but does not gate completion under the current locked protocol).
- **Adherence** = complete days ÷ (rolling 30 trading days − excluded days). ≥ 95% required.
- A trading day with no post and no `NO SESSION` marker counts as a **miss**.

Commit the channel export in `/admin` → "Pre-market routine · Criterion 03".
